## 1. THE FUNDAMENTAL GENIE RULES

### 1.1 The Basics
| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **One genie per human** | Every person on Earth gets exactly one genie with one wish. No exceptions. |
| **Wishes are permanent** | Once granted, a wish cannot be undone by the wisher. |
| **Wishes are literal** | Genies interpret wishes exactly as spoken. Clever wording = clever results. Sloppy wording = unintended consequences. |
| **Last wish wins** | When contradictory wishes clash, the most recent wish takes precedence. (Critical for finale.) |
| **Genies are a hive mind** | All genies share knowledge. Any genie can answer any question truthfully. They cannot lie but can decline to answer. |
| **Genies know the count** | Genies know exactly how many wishes remain unused globally. This number decreases as people die unwished. |

### 1.2 Contradictory Wishes
When two wishes directly oppose each other:
- **If simultaneous:** They cancel out (mutual destruction)
- **If sequential:** Last wish wins (the newer wish overwrites the older)
- **If impossible to resolve:** Universe finds loopholes (wishes partially fulfilled in unexpected ways)

**Example:** Person A wishes "I am immortal." Person B wishes "Person A dies." If B wishes second, A dies. The "last wish wins" rule is absolute.

### 1.3 Wish Transfers
| Scenario | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Genie follows the wisher** | Your genie stays with you until you wish |
| **Giving away your genie** | Possible but irreversible. You forfeit your wish forever. |
| **Stealing a genie** | Not possible. Genies cannot be taken by force. |
| **Inheriting a genie** | When someone dies unwished, their genie becomes "unclaimed" and enters the Wishpool |

### 1.4 Wish-Protected Objects & Spaces
- A wish can protect a location (like The Lampwick) or object (like the bus)
- Protection means: immune to other wishes, immune to the Scouring
- **Visual rule:** Protected things have a faint **golden shimmer** that flares when tested
- **Limitation:** Protection doesn't extend beyond the object's boundaries. Step outside = vulnerable.

## 2. FLOYD'S WISH - THE IDEA MAN

### 2.1 The Exact Wording
> **"I wish that everyone thinks my ideas are good."**

This is **perception manipulation**, NOT reality bending. Floyd's ideas are NOT actually good—people just BELIEVE they are.

### 2.2 How It Works
| Aspect | Mechanic |
|--------|----------|
| **Scope** | Global. Affects everyone who hears Floyd's ideas. |
| **Effect** | Listeners experience genuine conviction that Floyd is brilliant. Not hypnosis—they truly believe. |
| **Duration** | Permanent while in Floyd's presence or under his influence. Can fade with distance/time if not reinforced. |
| **Reinforcement** | Hope's Hollow constantly reinforces the effect through: broadcasts, group meetings, the "tablet" signing ritual |

### 2.3 What Floyd's Wish CANNOT Do
- **Cannot make his ideas actually work** (his Scouring "solution" is fundamentally flawed)
- **Cannot force actions** (people believe his ideas are good but retain free will on whether to ACT)
- **Cannot create loyalty** (they like his IDEAS, not necessarily HIM)
- **Cannot override direct sensory evidence** (if someone SEES Floyd murder someone, the wish can't erase that)

### 2.4 Floyd's Fatal Flaw (Key to Defeat)
Floyd is **addicted to recognition**. He doesn't actually care if his ideas are good—he needs to be SEEN as the genius who had them.

**The hollow center:** Deep down, Floyd knows the recognition is artificial. Everyone "thinks" his ideas are good because of magic, not because they ARE good. This gnaws at him.

**Defeat mechanism:** When Floyd is forced to confront that his recognition is hollow—that nobody actually believes in HIM, only the wish—his conviction cracks. A man whose entire identity is "the Idea Man" cannot survive realizing he has no real ideas.

## 3. IMMUNITY RULES

### 3.1 Why Daisy Can Resist
**Root cause:** Daisy's core flaw is **analysis paralysis**—she cannot commit to believing anything fully.

| Factor | How It Creates Immunity |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Dating history** | She dated Floyd and never bought his bullshit even then. Pattern established. |
| **Psychological makeup** | Her inability to commit extends to convictions. She can't fully "believe" Floyd's ideas are good because she can't fully believe anything. |
| **Scanner anomaly** | The scanner at HH entry flags her as a "glitch" or "anomaly"—something genetically/psychologically resistant to conviction-manipulation. This is the visual breadcrumb. |

**Thematic payoff:** Daisy's FLAW (paralysis) becomes her STRENGTH (immunity). She wins not despite her indecision but BECAUSE of it.

### 3.2 Why Alex Can See Through
**His wish:** Alex wished for the ability to **see the truth**.

| Application | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| **Against Floyd** | Alex can see that people are under a spell. He sees the "wrongness" in their conviction. |
| **Limitation** | He can see the lie but can't break others free from it. |
| **Visual** | When Alex looks at brainwashed people, he sees something "off"—a faint discoloration, a glitch in their expressions, like a filter is overlaid on them. |

### 3.3 Why Robbie Gets ULTRA Brainwashed
**The vulnerability:** Robbie's entire identity is built on BELIEVING in heroes. His superpower comes from faith in heroic ideals.

When Floyd presents himself as a hero-savior, Robbie's wish-enhanced belief system makes him MORE susceptible, not less. His strength becomes his weakness.

**The recovery:** Robbie breaks free not through logic (Alex's domain) or doubt (Daisy's domain), but through his **moral compass**. When Floyd's actions cross a line that violates Robbie's hero code, the spell shatters.

### 3.4 How the Spell Breaks (For Everyone)
The spell doesn't "shatter" universally through one event. It breaks **individually** based on each person's breaking point:

1. **Direct contradiction:** Seeing Floyd do something undeniably evil (he claims to save humanity while killing Ed)
2. **Personal connection:** Someone they trust (Daisy, Alex) presenting evidence while demonstrating genuine concern
3. **Recognition of the hollow:** Realizing Floyd's ideas have FAILED (the Scouring isn't stopping, people are still dying)

**Finale:** The mass awakening happens when Floyd is publicly exposed AND his "solution" visibly fails simultaneously.

## 4. THE SCOURING

### 4.1 What It Is
The Scouring is a **cascading reality collapse** caused by too many contradictory wishes existing simultaneously.

- **Visual:** A wave of golden-white destruction that disintegrates everything in its path
- **Sound:** A roar like reality tearing, punctuated by the screams of wishes being unmade
- **Effect:** Kills all non-protected life, destroys all non-protected structures, leaves behind scorched earth

### 4.2 The Mechanics
| Aspect | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **Cause** | Wish-overload. Too many contradictions create "knots" in reality. The Scouring is reality "smoothing out" by erasing the knots. |
| **Direction** | Scourings radiate outward from points of maximum contradiction. They follow population density. |
| **Speed** | Fast enough to be terrifying, slow enough to create chase sequences (think: pyroclastic flow) |
| **Survivability** | Only wish-protected spaces survive. The Lampwick, certain habitats, protected vehicles. |

### 4.3 Floyd's Role
**The truth:** Floyd is not CAUSING the Scourings, but he is WEAPONIZING them.

| Floyd's Belief | Reality |
|----------------|---------|
| "I'm the only one who can stop the Scouring" | He cannot. His ideas are magically perceived as good but aren't actually good. |
| "I'm collecting genies to save humanity" | He's collecting genies to have maximum power when the Scouring ends |
| "Hope's Hollow will be the last place standing" | Partially true—but only because of wish-protection, not because his plan works |

**The tragic irony:** Floyd genuinely believes he's the hero. His wish makes everyone ELSE believe it too. But he can't stop the Scouring because his "good ideas" are actually terrible. He's leading humanity off a cliff while everyone applauds.

### 4.4 The Ticking Clock
Use consistent language throughout:

- **Act 1:** "Three Scourings have hit. Each one is closer."
- **Early Act 2:** "Two Scourings left before it reaches the Midwest."
- **Mid Act 2:** "Next Scouring hits in 72 hours."
- **Act 3:** "The final Scouring is coming. This one ends it all."

Show tracking visually: news broadcasts with maps, Floyd's war room with projections, genie updates on global wish-counts.

## 5. THE WISHPOOL

### 5.1 What It Is
The Wishpool is a **metaphysical reservoir** where unclaimed genies gather.

- **Visual:** A massive column of swirling light beneath Hope's Hollow, like a contained aurora
- **Sound:** A chorus of whispered voices—the genies waiting to be claimed
- **Location:** Underground, accessed through Floyd's compound

### 5.2 How Genies Enter
When someone dies without making a wish, their genie becomes "unclaimed" and is drawn to the nearest concentration of genie-energy. Floyd has created the largest concentration, so unclaimed genies flow to his Wishpool.

**The Scouring connection:** Every Scouring kills thousands of unwished people, sending their genies to Floyd's pool.

### 5.3 Floyd's Use
Floyd draws power from the Wishpool by making **aggregate wishes**—using the collective power of thousands of unclaimed genies to amplify his own capabilities.

| Capability | How |
|------------|-----|
| **Broadcast range** | Wishpool power extends his "good ideas" perception globally |
| **Protection radius** | Wishpool powers Hope's Hollow's shields |
| **The Tablet ritual** | When residents sign the tablet, their genie joins the pool, giving Floyd control |

### 5.4 Freeing the Wishpool (Tim's Role)
**The problem:** The Wishpool is "dammed"—genies are trapped, unable to return to their rightful owners or pass on naturally.

**Tim's solution:** Tim's plumbing knowledge isn't literally about pipes—it's about **FLOW**. He understands pressure systems, blockages, and release valves.

**The Water Hammer:** In plumbing, a water hammer is a pressure surge when flow is suddenly stopped. Tim realizes the Wishpool is like a backed-up system. By creating a "water hammer" effect (sudden shock to the genie-flow), he can burst the dam.

**Setup required:** Plant Tim examining "flow patterns" in strange places throughout Act 2. He notices the genie-energy behaves like a fluid system. His plumber instincts kick in.

**Payoff:** In Act 3, Tim sacrifices himself to trigger the Water Hammer, releasing all captured genies simultaneously.

## 6. REMNANTS

### 6.1 What They Are
Remnants are people who have been **wished back from death**.

- They are real, conscious, functional humans
- They are **existentially dependent** on their wisher's survival
- They have a subtle "uncanny valley" quality—something slightly off

### 6.2 Rules
| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Dependency** | If the wisher dies, the remnant vanishes instantly |
| **Awareness** | Remnants know they are remnants. They remember dying. |
| **Physicality** | They are fully physical—can eat, sleep, be hurt |
| **Limitation** | Cannot have their own genie (they already had one and died) |

### 6.3 Visual Language
Remnants have a faint **blue-white aura** (remnant dust) clinging to them. Visible in certain light. Becomes more obvious when stressed.

- June has this aura (wished back by Ed)
- "Clone Daisy" has this aura (wished into existence by Floyd)—this immediately tells Alex she's not the real Daisy

### 6.4 Emotional Rules
Remnants exist on **borrowed time** and know it. This makes their love purer—they choose to be present knowing they could vanish any moment.

June's arc: She doesn't waste her borrowed existence on fear. She uses it to be the mother Robbie needs, even knowing it's finite.

## 7. WILL'S SECRET

### 7.1 The Truth
Will is a **former genie** who was rewarded with human life after saving Earth from a previous G-Day (Genie Day) centuries ago.

### 7.2 Implications
| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **How does he have a genie?** | When he became human, he was given a human's birthright—one genie, one wish. He hasn't used it. |
| **Why does he know so much?** | He lived through this before. He's seen what wishes do to humanity. |
| **Why stay in the bar?** | The Lampwick is built on a **nexus point**—the same spot where the original Wishpool formed. It has inherent protection. |
| **His wish** | Will wished to protect The Lampwick and everyone inside from all wishes. This is why the bar survives everything. |

### 7.3 Finale Role
The finale takes place at The Lampwick—the **only thing left standing** when everything else has fallen.

Will's reveal: He confesses his nature when hope seems lost. He's been waiting for someone worthy to help—someone who reminded him of why he saved humanity the first time.

Will's sacrifice/contribution: He offers Daisy guidance on how to phrase her final wish. He's seen thousands of wishes go wrong. He knows how to make one go right.

## 8. DAISY'S FINAL WISH

### 8.1 The Setup Requirements
For Daisy's wish to feel earned, we need:
1. **Her genie relationship:** Daisy and her genie have bonded throughout. The genie is a CHARACTER, not a tool.
2. **Her arc completion:** She must move from paralysis to DECISIVE ACTION.
3. **Stakes clarity:** The wish must address the global threat, not just protect Robbie.

### 8.2 The Wish Logic
Daisy cannot wish "stop the Scouring" (too vague, genies can't undo physics).
Daisy cannot wish "kill Floyd" (last wish wins—Floyd could counter).
Daisy cannot wish "undo all wishes" (would kill all remnants including June, and remove all protections).

**The clever angle:** Daisy must wish for something that USES the rules rather than breaks them.

### 8.3 The Optimal Wish (Proposed)
> **"I wish that everyone can see the truth about every wish that's been made."**

**Why this works:**
- Uses "last wish wins" (supersedes Floyd's perception manipulation)
- Gives EVERYONE Alex's gift globally
- People can now see that Floyd's ideas are actually terrible
- People can see the Scouring's real cause
- People can see what they wished for and what it actually cost
- Mass awakening happens organically, not through exposition

**Why this is clever:**
- Daisy—the woman who couldn't commit to anything—commits to TRUTH
- She doesn't force anyone to act; she gives them the information to CHOOSE
- It's not violence; it's illumination
- Floyd's power crumbles because his wish relied on people NOT seeing truth

**Why this protects Robbie:**
- Robbie broke from Floyd when he SAW Floyd's evil
- Global truth-vision ensures everyone can see what Robbie saw
- They'll protect him because they'll see his goodness
- She's not wishing FOR Robbie; she's wishing so the world can see Robbie for who he is

### 8.4 The Genie Reunion
After the wish, Daisy and her genie have a moment. The genie can finally go—but they've become friends. The goodbye is emotional, earned, and bittersweet.
