## Dialogue Craft

Characters live — they don't narrate.

Not: "Your ideas aren't good, Floyd."
But: She checks her phone mid-proposal.

Before any line: Does it serve the character or the audience? Could anyone say it?

---

## Craft Rules

- **Subtext over exposition** — Trust the audience
- **No cruft** — Only what's needed
- **PG-13** — Spielbergian, not graphic
- **Arcs in every scene** — No "placeholder" scenes
